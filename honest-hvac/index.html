What We Do

SERVICES.

HVAC, plumbing, and home diagnostics for Baltimore homeowners. Every job starts with the truth, not a sales pitch.

01
AC Restoration & Deep Cleaning

Before spending thousands on a new unit, let us restore yours. Dirty coils and worn components can make a healthy system perform like a dead one.

  • Evaporator & condenser coil cleaning
  • Drain line flush & treatment
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Fan & blower inspection
  • Performance testing post-service
02
HVAC Repair & Diagnostics

Not cooling? Not heating? Strange sounds? We run a full diagnostic before touching anything — so the repair we recommend is the one you actually need.

  • Complete system diagnostic
  • Compressor & motor testing
  • Electrical component inspection
  • Ductwork assessment
  • Honest repair vs. replace recommendation
03
Thermostat Installation & Repair

A bad thermostat makes a perfectly good system act broken. We diagnose, replace, or upgrade — smart home setups included.

  • Smart thermostat install (Nest, Ecobee, etc.)
  • Wiring troubleshooting
  • Manual-to-programmable upgrades
  • HVAC compatibility check included
04
Plumbing Service Calls

Leaking pipes, low pressure, running toilets, slow drains. We find the real problem and fix it. No guessing, no upsells.

  • Leak detection & repair
  • Drain cleaning & clog removal
  • Faucet & fixture repair/replacement
  • Water heater inspection
  • Emergency plumbing calls
05
Home System Assessments

Our signature service. A full mechanical walkthrough of your property. Clear findings. Written summary. Zero pressure to buy anything.

  • HVAC system health check
  • Plumbing inspection
  • Electrical overview
  • Written summary of findings
  • No-pressure repair recommendations
06
Property Maintenance Programs

For landlords and investors who want a proactive partner. Stay ahead of problems before they become emergencies.

  • Scheduled seasonal checkups
  • Priority response for enrolled properties
  • Multi-unit property support
  • Maintenance logs provided

The Story Behind Honest HVAC

Built on Frustration.
Driven by Integrity.

Desean Matthews Graham started Honest HVAC Plumbing & Home Diagnostic after watching the same thing happen over and over in Baltimore homes: a homeowner calls a contractor, gets told they need a full replacement, spends $5,000–$10,000, and finds out later the original unit could have been fixed for a few hundred dollars.

That’s not an oversight. That’s the model. Contractors get paid more when they sell you new equipment. So they find reasons to justify it.

“I treat your property’s systems like machines that deserve to be saved, not just replaced for a quick commission.”

— Desean Matthews Graham, Owner

As a Home Diagnostic Specialist℠, Desean’s process on every job starts with one thing: find out what’s actually wrong. Not a 10-minute walkthrough that ends with a pitch — a real diagnostic, with real findings, explained to you plainly.

If your system can be fixed, he’ll fix it. If it genuinely needs to be replaced, he’ll explain exactly why and show you the evidence. No pressure. No games. Just the truth about your home.
