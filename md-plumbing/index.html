01
Water Heater Repair & Replacement
No hot water is an emergency. We diagnose, repair, or replace tank and tankless water heaters — often same day.
  • Tank & tankless water heaters
  • Anode rod replacement (powered & magnesium)
  • Odor diagnostics — rotten egg smell from well water
  • Emergency same-day replacement
02
Drain Cleaning & Unclogging
Slow or blocked drains fixed fast. Kitchen, bathroom, and main line service with no mess left behind.
  • Kitchen & bathroom drain cleaning
  • Main sewer line cleaning
  • Camera inspection for stubborn blockages
  • Preventative drain maintenance
03
New Pipe Installation
Old galvanized steel, corroded copper, or burst PVC — we handle full replacements and new installs.
  • Copper, PVC & PEX piping
  • Whole-home repipe projects
  • Outdoor hose bib installation
  • Leak detection & repair
04
Sump Pumps & Sewage Ejectors
Protect your basement and property with properly installed and maintained pump systems.
  • Sump pump installation & replacement
  • Battery backup systems
  • Sewage ejector pit service
  • Discharge line repair
05
Garbage Disposal & Kitchen Fixtures
Fast installation and repair for garbage disposals, kitchen faucets, and under-sink connections.
  • Garbage disposal installation & repair
  • Kitchen faucet replacement
  • Under-sink plumbing repair
  • Dishwasher connection service
06
Bathroom Fixtures & Repairs
Toilets, sinks, showers, tubs — everything in your bathroom handled by one trusted pro.
  • Toilet repair & replacement
  • Showerhead & valve repair
  • Bathroom faucet installation
  • Bidet installation
07
Emergency Plumbing — 24/7
Burst pipes, flooding, shut-off failures — we’re available any time, any day. No extra runaround.
  • Burst pipe emergency repair
  • Flooding response
  • Shut-off valve failures
  • Same-day service across Baltimore area
08
Home Plumbing Assessments
Buying a home or own an older property? We walk through your plumbing system and give you a straight condition report.
  • Pre-purchase plumbing inspection
  • Water pressure testing
  • Pipe condition assessment
  • Written findings & recommendations
